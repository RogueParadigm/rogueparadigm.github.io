The game

Each time you level up, you can pick a new weapon, upgrade one you already have or take a passive upgrade for your snake.

Game modes

Expedition is the current game mode. You explore the Scrapyard and destroy hordes of machines. Some points of interest give you upgrades. Others let you change the pace of the run.

I’d like to try other modes during Early Access, like a wave-based mode or one that plays more like a roguelike. Player feedback will help me decide which ones to keep and which ones to cut.

Weapons, snakes, upgrades and drivers

I’ll add new weapons, snakes and drivers and keep balancing the game with each update.

Biomes

The Scrapyard is the first biome. I’m aiming for at least four in total by the end of Early Access.

Got an idea for a game mode? Come tell me about it on Discord.

Frequently asked questions

Why Early Access?

I want to balance weapons and upgrades based on how your runs play out. I’d also like to try other game modes. Your feedback will help me decide which ones to keep.

How long will Early Access last?

I expect Early Access to last about a year. That estimate may change depending on how the game mode trials go and how long it takes to finish the biomes.

How will the full version differ?

I’m aiming for at least four biomes in total for version 1.0, including the Scrapyard. I also plan to add weapons, snakes and drivers. I’d like to try wave-based combat and a mode that plays more like a roguelike.

What’s in the current build?

The current build includes a full run in the Scrapyard. You choose your route and where to mount weapons on your snake. Some places offer bonuses, while others change how the run unfolds. Between runs, you can change your loadout and unlock permanent upgrades in the garage.

Will the price change after Early Access?

Yes. The price will go up when version 1.0 comes out.

How can I take part?

You can reach me on the studio Discord or in the Steam discussions. Tell me if a weapon feels too strong or too weak. You can also suggest a game mode or let me know what you thought of the ones you’ve tried.
