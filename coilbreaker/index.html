Arrow keys, WASD or the left stick to steer. With a finger or the mouse: hold down and slide, a stick appears under it. Weapons fire on their own. Collect XP cells to level up. Pick a card by tapping it, with the mouse, or with the arrows and Enter. Esc or Start pauses.

The garage, the other snakes and weapons, Steam achievements and further stages are in the full game.

FULL GAME / MOUNTING A WEAPON

THE FULL GAME

Coming soon to Steam

Your snake is a convoy of scrap: a head, body blocks and a tail. At each level-up you pick a weapon, a rank or a passive, and where to bolt each new weapon. A machine gun on the head fires ahead of you; on the tail, it fires behind.

The horde keeps growing and bosses arrive on schedule. Between runs, you spend your scrap in the garage, unlock other snakes and weapons, and earn Steam achievements.

Early Access starts with the Scrapyard. More stages will come with updates, starting with the Foundry.
