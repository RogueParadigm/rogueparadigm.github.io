THE STEAM DEMO

The Scrapyard, through to extraction

Your snake is a convoy of scrap: a head, body blocks and a tail. At each level-up you pick a weapon, a rank or a passive, and where to bolt each new weapon. A machine gun on the head fires ahead of you; on the tail, it fires behind.

The demo includes the Scrapyard, three snakes, five weapons and four unlockable pilots. Survive the hordes and bosses until extraction at twenty minutes. Between runs, spend scrap on the first rank of garage upgrades.

Sentinelle guides you in French or English. Interface and subtitles support ten languages. The full game adds snakes, weapons, Freya and her frogs, higher garage ranks and Steam achievements. Early Access starts with the Scrapyard.
