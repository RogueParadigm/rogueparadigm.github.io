Description

COILBREAKER is a survivor roguelite: you steer, your weapons fire on their own. Your snake is a convoy of scrap: a head, body blocks and a tail. At each level-up you pick a weapon, a rank or a passive, and where to bolt each new weapon. A machine gun on the head fires ahead of you; on the tail, it fires behind.

The horde keeps growing and bosses arrive on schedule. Between runs, you spend your scrap in the garage and unlock other snakes and weapons.

Features

  • Where each weapon sits on the snake (head, body or tail) changes how it fires.
  • Runs of about 20 minutes, with bosses on schedule and an overload phase at the end.
  • Between runs: garage upgrades, new snakes and weapons to unlock, Steam achievements.
  • 2D, seen from above, made with Godot.

Early Access

Early Access starts with one stage, the Scrapyard. The second, the Foundry, will come in a later update. Version 1.0 is planned with two new stages, new weapons and new bosses.

Use of AI

Generative AI was used to create and edit visual assets and to assist translations into English, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. Sentinelle’s French and English voice lines are pre-generated audio files made with ElevenLabs and, for some lines, local speech synthesis tools using that voice as a reference. These assets are selected, edited and integrated during development. No content is generated by AI during gameplay.
