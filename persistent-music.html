Elys Persistent Music

One music track that keeps playing across level changes.

Start a track once and let it follow the player between maps. Change the music with a crossfade, pause or resume it, and control its volume from Blueprint.

Getting started

Make it work in your project.

  1. After publication, get the free plugin from Fab and enable it in your project.
  2. Call Play Music in Blueprint and choose your music asset.
  3. Travel to another level: the track keeps playing.
